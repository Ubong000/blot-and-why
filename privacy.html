PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 8 November 2026

What this app is

Blot Uploader is a private desktop tool that its operator runs on their own computer to upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channel, Blot & Why. It has no other users.

Data the app accesses

With the operator's permission, the app uses Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API to identify the operator's channel and to upload videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to it. It does not read or collect data about viewers or other Google users.

Storage and sharing

The access token is stored only on the operator's computer. The app has no server, sends data only to Google and YouTube, and does not sell or share data with anyone else.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions. Deleting the local token file also removes the app's stored access.
